How to play

Tap a square to toggle a note. Choose Accent for a louder hit, or hold Shift while clicking. On a keyboard, use arrow keys to move through the grid, Space or Enter to toggle, A to accent, and Delete to erase. Click a track’s name to mute it. A and B hold separate bars; turn on A → B to play them in sequence. Undo restores edits, clear actions, imports, and loaded presets.

Headphones welcome. Start with Quiet and use your device’s volume control. Playback stops when this page is hidden or your audio is interrupted. Browser and device timing can vary.
